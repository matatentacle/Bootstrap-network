Tenant-facing letters should be short and non-technical: three short paragraphs, one concrete detail (for example the IP address) to show Bootstrap knows the setup, then the one question being asked. No tables of VLANs, ports or internal addresses.

**Why:** on 14 Oct 2026 Mat rejected the first external-IP letters as "simply everything too much technical detail". The readers are office managers, not IT people, and the goal is one answer (do you still need it), not education.
**How to apply:** for any letter or notice to tenants, lead with why we are writing, state what they have in one or two plain sentences, ask the question with a date, and sign off. Keep the technical detail for Mat's own notes. See [[external-ip-fact-finding]].

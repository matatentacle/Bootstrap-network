When sourcing kit or prices for Mat, use these suppliers first, in Mat's words his preferred ones (14 Oct 2026): **Cable Monkey** (cablemonkey.co.uk), **Broadband Buyer** (broadbandbuyer.com), **FS** (fs.com), **Canford** (canford.co.uk). **Ebuyer** is accepted as a mainstream fallback after Mat agreed to it on 14 Oct 2026. Do not link eBay, Amazon marketplace sellers, Ubuy, or small unknown resellers (Avoira, Teqex and similar were rejected).

**Why:** Mat said "always use reputable suppliers when working with me, I don't buy from random marketplace type sellers" after I listed console cables from small resellers, then gave the preferred list.
**How to apply:** search the four preferred suppliers first; fall back to Ebuyer or the manufacturer's own store; drop any cheaper unknown seller even if it wins on price. State the supplier on every line of a shopping list. See [[mat-operator]] and [[bootstrap-miro-board]].
